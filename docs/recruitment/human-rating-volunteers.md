# DRAFT: call for human rating volunteers

> **DRAFT FOR PROJECT-OWNER APPROVAL. Do not publish or send.** It may be used only after the
> owner has approved the consent text (`docs/templates/rating-consent.txt`) and reissued the
> packets with it. The owner fills in the placeholders in angle brackets.

---

**Help test whether AI can judge the quality of long talks: careful readers wanted**

AI Video Discovery is an open-source research project. It tries to rate podcasts and videos on
what they contain (depth, accuracy, clarity, structure and so on) instead of how popular they
are. Automated ratings mean nothing until they are compared with careful, independent human
judgement, and right now the project has none.

**What you would do**

* Receive **one HTML file** by <channel, for example e-mail>. It opens in any modern browser,
  needs no account or installation, and works without an internet connection. It cannot send
  anything anywhere.
* Read one practice episode: a transcript of a public technical podcast, published by Changelog
  Media under CC BY-SA 4.0.
* Rate it on ten aspects using the written description of each score, and cite the passages
  that support your rating. You can always answer that the transcript does not show something.
* Press **Download ratings file** and send that file back **privately** to the coordinator.

**Time.** About 50–65 minutes for the practice episode. This is an estimate, not a measurement.
Your packet records how long you spent, which tells us the real figure. If you want to continue
afterwards, each further episode takes about 50–85 minutes. You choose how many, and when.

**Blind and independent.** You will not see any automated rating or any other volunteer's
ratings, and please do not look for them or discuss episodes with other raters while rating.
That independence is what makes the comparison meaningful.

**Voluntary.** Unpaid. You can stop at any time without giving a reason, and send partial work
or nothing.

**Privacy.** You are identified by a pseudonym. The coordinator knows which person uses which
pseudonym and keeps that private. The file opens with the full consent text, which explains what
is recorded, that your individual ratings stay private (only aggregates may be published, and
publishing individual ratings would need your separate agreement), how long files are kept
and how to withdraw. You can download your ratings only after you have read and agreed to it.

**Returning your file.** Send it only to the coordinator, through <private channel>. **Do not
post it in a GitHub issue, a pull request or anywhere public:** it contains a code that
identifies your packet.

You need to be at least 18 and comfortable reading long English transcripts on software, AI or
product work. No subject expertise is required.

Interested? Contact <coordinator contact>.
