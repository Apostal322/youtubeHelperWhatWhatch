Rating packet

Rater . Read each transcript, then rate every dimension on the right. A status is required for each dimension; a number never is. If the transcript does not show it, say so: an unavailable dimension is not a low score. Cite evidence by selecting words in a segment, then pressing Cite under the dimension. You will not see any automated assessment of these items; please do not look for one.

Your work is saved in this browser as you go (if the browser allows it). When you are done, press Download ratings file and send that file back to the coordinator. You can stop and come back. Expect about an hour per item: the transcripts are long.

Usability pilot. This packet holds an excerpt (the opening part of one episode), to test whether these instructions and choices are clear and how long rating takes. Rate only what the excerpt shows. Your answers are never used as a rating of the episode. Please write down anything that was unclear: that is what this pilot is for.

Before you start: consent

Your ratings can only be downloaded after you agree. You can stop at any time.
